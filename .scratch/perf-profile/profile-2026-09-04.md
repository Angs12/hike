## 6. Per-binary conclusions

- **sort**: one sub (sub_4d2a) is 76.6% of producer time; of its 10.4s, ~7.4s is
  `backward_slice` (Def.Set fixpoint over ~3192 relevant defs) and ~2.75s is a
  6000-iteration fixpoint that produces nothing (degraded arm). Fixing the
  backward set cost has the single biggest prize; the non-convergence burn is
  second (and yields zero tags).
- **grep**: cost is spread (top sub 41%); sub_e350 (converged, 4.57s) splits
  32% analyze / 67% fixpoint — both lanes matter; the fixpoint lane there is
  the transfer+refinement mix of §4/§5.

## 7. Follow-up optimization candidates (ordered by expected payoff)

1. **Backward-slice set/combinator cost** (prize: ~40% of total run, ~70% of
   worst-sub producer). Drivers, all visible at `src/hike_vsa_relevance.ml`:
   per-transfer `initial_vars` fold over the whole R-sized `rel` set (:185-188);
   `Core.Set.mem/add` on `Def.Set` keyed by BAP term comparison (perf leaves:
   `Bap_ir.compare`, `Bap_var.compare`, String/Int compares) with R≈3192;
   `enqueued @ rest` list append per added def (:207, quadratic in block defs);
   `Core.Set.to_list new_vars` per step (:200); `producer_of`/`def_uses`
   map finds per var (perf: `Map.find_exn` ~8% in-phase); Graphlib round-robin
   rounds × 655 blocks with full-set `equal` compares each round (:216-220).
   Shapes: key `rel` by tid (int compare), hoist/maintain `initial_vars`
   incrementally, difference-list worklist, memoize the static producer map,
   replace round-robin with a worklist.
2. **Non-convergence burn** (prize: 2.75s/sub-class, 100% waste — degraded arm
   emits Unbounded tags). The 6000-iteration cap (`src/cbat_vsa/cbat_vsa.ml:2860`)
   × per-visit refine cost. Needs precision work (landmark/consumption tuning;
   `conv_diag` on sub_4d2a reports only "NOT CONVERGED after 6000 rounds, no gap
   pair"); even a cheaper tripwire (e.g. detect oscillation earlier) must stay
   sound — measure only, no shape prescribed here.
3. **Inline edge refinement** (prize: ~70% of fixpoint samples). Drivers:
   nested contextual fixpoint per edge (`~steps:256`, always truncating —
   WALKS truncs == calls); `reverse_def_walk` re-`denote_def`s every live def
   (ml:1484-1486) instead of reusing the memoised transfer; `def_constraints`
   + `WordSet.meet` per operand. Shapes: memoize `post_v` per (block,def) under
   the solution version; skip refinement when the seed is TOP; adaptive step cap.
4. **Allocation churn / GC** (~8% of run; 212M minor words per 0.9s sub):
   Clp/Zahlen big-int ops (`ml_z_addsub/shift/logand/compare` in leaves) and
   AI env joins allocate heavily. Reduce temp allocation in
   `Clp.intersection/extrapolate` + `AI.join` paths.
5. **`caml_apply2` 10-14% self**: closure-heavy plumbing (Graphlib `transfer`/`f`
   closures, `xen` wrappers). Structural; flambda already on (bap-flambda
   switch). Low actionability — listed for completeness.
6. **Forward lane (1.4-3%)**: leave alone.

## 8. Method notes (for the next profiler)

- `perf record` needs paranoid ≤ 2; was 3 (all events refused, even
  `task-clock`), now -1. Always `cat /proc/sys/kernel/perf_event_paranoid` first.
- Hybrid-CPU trap: default `cycles` resolved to `cpu_atom/cycles/P` → **50
  samples** for a 40s run. Use `-e cpu_core/cycles/P -e cpu_atom/cycles/P`
  (→ 25,485 samples). `--call-graph dwarf,8192` keeps perf.data manageable.
- `dune exec` forks: the child (not `$!`) is the probe; select by
  `/proc/PID/exe` symlink, else you profile/attach to the idle dune parent.
- gdb on OCaml with `--readnever`: only frame #0 is trustworthy (deeper frames
  are unwinder garbage — small-int addresses like `0x3`). Leaf-PC sampling
  (`bt 1`) or exact-symbol breakpoints work; `rbreak` regexes don't support `+`.
- `collect_def_maps`/`detect_dynamic_alloc` have no `T` symbols (inlined into
  `analyze_2559`); `forward_vars_1077`, `backward_slice_1752`, `d_of_defs_1125`,
  `block_contributors_1813`, `static_graph_vsa_13819` do.
- `perf report` prints one section per PMU event; add the cpu_core + cpu_atom
  sections (same shape here).
